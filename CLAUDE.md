## Design
- Colours: VLS Red #AE1015, Charcoal #161616, Ivory #FAF8F5, Gold #C8A46B.
- Sans body; restrained serif for select headings/quotes. Editorial, spacious, crisp dividers.
- Variance 6/10, motion 3/10, density 5/10.
- No gradient headlines, fake 3D gavels, animated scales of justice, AI-generated courtroom art, or wall-to-wall identical cards.
- Hero: asymmetric split on desktop (message left, real photo right, CTA visible). Mobile: headline + CTA early, compact sticky bottom registration action.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Visible focus states. Labels on every field.
